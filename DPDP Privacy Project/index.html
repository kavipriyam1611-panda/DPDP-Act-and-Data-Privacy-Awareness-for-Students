Where the law stands (checked 22 Oct 2026)

The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025. The Data Protection Board part began immediately. Consent manager rules start around 13 November 2026. Most other duties, including the rights on the My rights page and the penalties, are due around 13 May 2027 (18 months after notification). Dates can change, so confirm on the MeitY website before you quote them.

My data rights under the DPDP Act

You are the Data Principal. The company or app using your data is the Data Fiduciary.

Right to information (Section 11)

Ask what personal data is processed, and who it was shared with.

Right to correction and erasure (Section 12)

Fix wrong data, complete it, update it, or ask for it to be erased when it is no longer needed.

Right to grievance redressal (Section 13)

Complain to the company first. If that fails, go to the Data Protection Board of India.

Right to nominate (Section 14)

Name someone to use your rights if you die or cannot act for yourself.

Withdraw consent (Section 6)

Withdrawing must be as easy as giving consent was.

Children

For anyone under 18, apps need verifiable parental consent. Tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted ads aimed at children are not allowed.

Your duties (Section 15)

Do not impersonate others, hide material facts, or file false complaints. Penalties can apply.

Using AI responsibly for this topic

AI is not legal advice

Use AI to understand a law in simple words. For decisions or disputes, read the official text or ask a qualified lawyer.

No real personal data in prompts

Never paste Aadhaar, phone numbers, marksheets or photos into a chatbot. Use made-up examples.

Flag outdated law summaries

Chatbots may describe the DPDP Act as “not yet in force” because they learned before the 2025 Rules. Check dates against MeitY.

Verify every claim

AI can invent section numbers and penalties. Cross-check each figure with the gazette text.

Reliable sources
